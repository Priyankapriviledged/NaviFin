Pre-arrival preparations

01

Relocation start

GDPR consent, questionnaire, pre-consultation if needed

  • Give GDPR consent to your relocation provider
  • Complete the intake questionnaire
  • Book a pre-consultation call if you have questions

02

Immigration

Residence permit application, employer-sponsored

  • Confirm your permit basis with your employer
  • Submit residence permit application or EU registration
  • Prepare supporting documents Migri requests

03 · 1–3 months

Permit decision & cards

Book flights only after receiving the cards

  • Track your application status
  • Receive your decision and residence permit cards
  • Only then book flight tickets

04

Pre-arrival call

With a local relocation consultant

  • Schedule your pre-arrival consultation
  • Review arrival logistics and open questions

05 · on arrival

Destination services

Housing support

  • Start housing search

After arrival in Finland

01 · 1–4 days

Local registration (DVV)

Finnish ID number, healthcare eligibility, address registration

  • Book a DVV appointment or visit a service point
  • Bring passport and residence permit card
  • Receive your Finnish personal ID number

02 · after ID

Tax card

Required before your first payroll run

  • Apply for your tax card via Vero
  • Share it with your employer before payroll

03 · 3–4 months

Social security (Kela)

Apply as soon as your ID number is ready

  • Submit your Kela application
  • Expect 3–4 months processing time

04 · 7–14 days

Bank account

Needs a registered address from DVV first

  • Book a bank appointment after DVV registration
  • Bring ID and proof of address

05 · parallel

Home search

More options open up once your ID number is issued

  • Start browsing while your permit is processing
  • More listings available once you have a Finnish ID

06 · ongoing

Enjoy Finland

Update your bank details with Kela and tax office · don't forget the sauna

  • Update bank details with Kela and tax office
  • Settle into everyday life
